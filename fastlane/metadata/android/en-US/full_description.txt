VineOS runs a complete, isolated Android OS inside your existing device - its own init, Zygote, SurfaceFlinger and more. You get a real second phone inside your phone.

Highlights:
- 100% free, open source (GPL-3.0), no ads, no telemetry
- 32-bit apps on arm64-only phones via QEMU user-mode emulation
- Multiple ROMs (7.1, 9, 11, 12) and multiple simultaneous instances
- Material You UI, per-instance resource limits, snapshots

Beta: first bootable ROM is in progress; daily driver readiness varies by device.
